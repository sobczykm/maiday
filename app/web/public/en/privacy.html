Privacy Policy

Effective from 24 October 2026. In case of discrepancies, the Polish version prevails.

This policy explains what personal data we process when you use Maiday at maiday.julqaj.com, why we do it, and what your rights are.

1. Data controller

The controller of your personal data is Mateusz Sobczyk, a private individual.
Contact for all data protection matters: maiday@sobczyk.dev.

2. What data we process

  • Google account data we receive when you sign in: your Google account identifier, name and email address. We never receive your password and we do not store your profile picture.
  • Content you enter yourself: tasks, habits, sick-day markers, notes, folders, labels, library items, job offers, custom tabs (tables, to-do lists, photo galleries), and uploaded images and files (e.g. PDFs, CVs).
  • Technical data: a session identifier stored in a cookie and short-lived server logs (IP address, date and time, requested address, browser information).

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Providing Maiday – creating and running your account, storing and displaying your content (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR – performance of a contract, i.e. the Terms).
  • Security and reliable operation – server logs, detecting abuse and errors (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – the controller's legitimate interest).
  • Backups – protecting your data against loss (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).
  • Handling your requests and complaints (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR).

We do not use your data for advertising, do not profile you, do not make automated decisions about you, and do not sell your data.

4. Sick days (health data)

Marking a day as a "sick day" in the habits module is information about your health, a special category of data. We process it only to show it to you and to keep your habit streaks intact, based on your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you give by marking such a day yourself. You can withdraw consent at any time by removing the marker; this does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing. Using this feature is optional.

5. Who processes data on our behalf

  • DigitalOcean, LLC – hosting of the application, database, files and backups. Servers and file storage are located in Frankfurt am Main (Germany, European Union). DigitalOcean processes data on our behalf under a data processing agreement.
  • Google – sign-in with a Google account. Google processes sign-in data as a separate controller, under the Google Privacy Policy.

We do not use analytics, advertising tools or third-party content delivery networks – fonts and icons are served from our own server.

6. Transfers outside the EEA

We store data in the European Union. DigitalOcean is a US company, so access to data from the US (e.g. for technical support) cannot be ruled out. Any such transfer is based on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses included in DigitalOcean's data processing agreement and, where applicable, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. You can request a copy of these safeguards at the address in section 1.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account data and your content – until you delete your account. You can delete individual entries and files in the app at any time.
  • When you delete your account, your data and files are removed immediately from the database and file storage. They may remain in the database backup archive for up to about 30 days, after which they are deleted automatically.
  • Server logs – for a short time, usually no longer than a few days.
  • Correspondence with us – for as long as needed to handle it and to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

8. Cookies and browser storage

We only use cookies that are strictly necessary for the service to work, so we do not ask for cookie consent:

  • maiday_session – keeps you signed in, for up to 30 days or until you sign out;
  • maiday_oauth_state – protects the Google sign-in process, for up to 10 minutes.

The app also stores view settings in your browser (localStorage), such as the last opened tab, filters or the collapsed sidebar.

The demo version (demo.maiday.julqaj.com) works without signing in and without an account. Everything you add there — entries, images and files — is stored only in your browser (localStorage and IndexedDB) and is not sent to our servers. You can remove it with “Clear demo data” or by clearing the site data in your browser.

9. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your data and receive a copy of it,
  • rectify your data,
  • erasure – the easiest way is Profile → Delete account ("Usuń konto"),
  • restrict processing,
  • data portability – in the app via Backup → Export data ("Kopia zapasowa → Eksportuj dane", a JSON file; images and files can be downloaded individually),
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest,
  • withdraw consent at any time (sick days).

To exercise your rights, write to maiday@sobczyk.dev. We will reply without undue delay and at the latest within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Polish supervisory authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, uodo.gov.pl), or with the authority in your country of residence.

Providing data is voluntary, but Maiday cannot be used without signing in with a Google account.

10. Security

Connections to Maiday are encrypted (HTTPS). Your content and files are available only when signed in to your account. The controller has technical access to the infrastructure and uses it only when necessary – to maintain and secure the service or to fulfil your request.

11. Changes to this policy

We will announce significant changes to this policy in the app. The current version is always available at maiday.julqaj.com/en/privacy.
